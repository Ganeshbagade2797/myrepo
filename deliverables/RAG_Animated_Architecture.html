Approved source: TRAVEL-v3, page 2: “For domestic business travel, meals are reimbursable up to INR 1,200 per day. Itemized receipts are required.”

Supported answer: “INR 1,500 exceeds the stated daily limit of INR 1,200. Itemized receipts are required [S1, p.2].” The source does not explain exception approvals or partial-claim handling.
